Privacy, in plain English

What we collect

Your first name, email, venue name and your nine answers, exactly as you entered them.

Why

To work out your pattern, show it to you, email you a copy, and follow up if you ask us to. If you left the newsletter box ticked, we also add you to the Food Game Weekly Wrap, and every issue has an unsubscribe link.

Where it lives

Your answers and contact details are stored in a secured database hosted in Sydney. A few trusted services help us deliver things: our email provider sends your result, our booking tool runs the free call, and our newsletter platform handles the Weekly Wrap if you asked for it. Some of those are based overseas. They act only on our instructions.

What we do not do

We do not sell your information, and we do not hand it to anyone for their own purposes. If we ever want to use it for something new, we will ask you first, and you can say no.

Your rights

Ask us what we hold, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, any time: julian@foodgamemedia.com.au. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles.
