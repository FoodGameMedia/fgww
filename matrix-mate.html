What it actually is

Every machine gets a record, and an agent that already read it.

Matrix Mate is Intelligent Equipment Management for hospitality. Every piece of equipment gets an IUM Tag — a durable QR sticker, no app to download, any smartphone, any staff member. Scanning it opens the IUM record for that machine, and MM8, the platform's agent, is already active on it.

MM8 is not an upsell. It is included on every IUM, in every tier, from day one, at no additional cost. It already holds the OEM baseline, the full service history, every fault ever logged and every repair ever made. It does not need to be briefed.

The part nobody costs

This is a design problem wearing an equipment costume.

Three costs, none of which ever appear on an invoice as themselves.

You always pay the emergency rate

No fault history means nothing to argue with. No documentation means no defence. The operator without data pays the premium rate, the overtime rate, and the parts rate they did not have to — every time, and never knowing by how much.

The knowledge leaves with the person

The one who knew which machine sulks in humidity, which supplier actually turns up, what was tried last time. When they go, it goes. The venue then relearns, at full price, what it already knew.

Compliance is a gap you cannot see

When a regulator, insurer or auditor asks for documented maintenance evidence, most operators cannot produce it. Not through negligence — because nothing was collecting it.

None of these are equipment problems. They are memory problems, which is why buying better gear never fixes them.

How it works

One tag. One scan. The record builds itself.

01

Tag every piece of equipment

A durable QR sticker on each item. From day one the platform retrieves the full OEM record — age, warranty status, service history — before the first scan is ever made.

02

Scan. MM8 activates.

It knows the equipment and guides the person through the right process for their context. A kitchen hand logging a fault is prompted differently to a technician arriving to fix one.

03

The right people know

The moment a fault is logged, the venue manager, operations director and owner are notified by email or SMS. No dashboard to check. No chasing. Every interaction sharpens the IUM record.

The Marketplace

One repair job. Multiple bids. The history already attached.

Route a fault to a preferred supplier already in the system, or open it to the Matrix Mate Marketplace and let registered technicians compete — each of them seeing the documented history before they quote.

No phone call describing a problem you can barely articulate. The technician arrives prepared, the cost is competitive, and the job is tracked from log to close, with every step added back to the IUM record.
